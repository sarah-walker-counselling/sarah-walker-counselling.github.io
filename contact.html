This site is work in progress.

It is important that you feel comfortable with your therapist, as the quality of the therapeutic relationship is key to successful work. Clients might meet with several therapists before choosing someone who feels the right fit.
I offer a free 20-minute phone or video consultation if you'd like to meet initially to discuss working together, and if you feel that we are a good match then we will arrange a first session at a time that works for you.
For our first consultation we will need to go through what is called 'contracting'. Basically I need to check that whoever I will be speaking with is able to understand what the session means.

Please send an email to sarahwalkercounselling@gmail.com if you have any further questions or would like to arrange an initial conversation. Please include in your email:

  • Your name
  • Your contact details
  • The name of the person needing counselling
  • A brief description of the issue
