About me

This site is work in progress.



Before qualifying as a counsellor, I spent several years in a corporate environment, working in a desk job in the city. After deciding to leave my position to start a family, I found myself reflecting on my experiences and feelings during this transitional phase. The time away from work allowed me to recognise a persistent sense of unease that I couldn’t quite articulate.

In seeking clarity, I began my own counselling journey. The safe and confidential space provided each week enabled me to explore my emotions deeply and gain valuable insights into my personal struggles. This process allowed me to view my experiences from new perspectives, facilitating significant personal growth and helping me move forward in areas where I had previously felt stuck.

Inspired by this transformative experience, I pursued training to become a counsellor myself. My goal is to create a non-judgmental and authentic environment for others, offering the same supportive space that helped me navigate my own challenges.
